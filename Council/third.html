Information

The Council isn't just a group—it's a community. Our website plays a crucial role in ensuring every student at BYUI feels connected and supported. By centralizing information and providing easy access to our activities, we aim to enhance participation, foster new friendships, and create a sense of belonging. Join Us! Whether you're seeking advice, looking to meet new friends, or simply want to laugh along with us, The Council welcomes you. Explore our website, join our meetings, and become part of a community where knowledge, camaraderie, and a good laugh are always on the agenda.
